# The interface

## The sidebar

The thin bar on the left is the **rail**. Each icon opens a panel: Hosts, Credentials, and whatever your plugins add, like Snippets or Tunnels. The bottom of the rail has Plugins, Settings and your account.

Drag icons to reorder them. Plugins you turn off take their icons with them.

Right-click an icon for more:

- **Open as tab** opens the panel as a tab. Ctrl+click or middle click does the same.
- **Open in right dock** keeps it open on the right, next to whatever you are doing.
- **Hide from sidebar** hides it. Bring it back from the same menu or in **Settings**, **Interface**.

## Tabs

Hosts, terminals, file managers, Manage and Settings all open as tabs along the top. Drag tabs to reorder them. Your open tabs come back when you sign in again, on any device. Turn that off in **Settings**, **Interface** with **Reopen Tabs on Login**.

## Split screen

Show up to six things side by side. Drag a tab onto another to split, or use **Split right** and **Split down** from a tab's menu.

- Drag a divider to resize. Double-click it to even them out.
- **Zoom pane** fills the split with one pane for a moment.
- **Change layout** picks a preset, like two side by side or a grid of four.
- While an empty pane is selected, anything you open from the sidebar opens there.

## How much is shown

Pick **Simple**, **Balanced** or **Advanced** in **Settings**, **Interface**. A preset only changes how much the UI shows. Every feature stays available. You can change any single setting after picking one.

## On a phone

On a narrow screen the rail becomes a bar at the bottom. **More** holds everything that doesn't fit. The desktop app always uses the full layout.

## Keyboard

- Press **Shift** twice to open the [command palette](/guide/command-palette).
- **Ctrl+Shift+F** toggles full screen.
- Change shortcuts in **Settings**, **Keybindings**.
