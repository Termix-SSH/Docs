# Delete your account

This page is for the Termix mobile app ("Termix - Remote Access" on Google Play), made by Luke Gustafson.

Termix accounts live on a Termix server that you or your organization runs. We do not run that server and do not hold your account, so the steps below delete it from your own server.

## Delete it in the app

1. Open Termix and sign in to the server.
2. Go to Settings, Accounts and sync.
3. Tap Delete server account.
4. Enter your password and tap Delete.

The account is deleted right away. This cannot be undone.

If you signed in with single sign-on (SSO), or you are the only admin on the server, the app cannot delete the account. Ask the person who runs your Termix server to delete it in Settings, Users.

## What is deleted

- Your account on the server
- Your hosts, logins, keys and settings on that server

Hosts and logins other people shared with you stay with their owners. The server's audit log may keep records of what your account did, for as long as the server's admin keeps that log.

## Delete some of your data

You can delete data without deleting your account:

- Hosts and logins: open one in the app and tap Delete. It is removed from the phone and the server right away.
- Data on the phone for one server: go to Settings, Accounts and sync, unlink the server and tap Delete from this phone. Nothing is deleted on the server.
- Everything on the phone: uninstall the app.
- Usage statistics: turn off Share anonymous usage statistics in Settings, Privacy and usage statistics. The app deletes anything it had waiting.

## Usage statistics

If you turned on anonymous usage statistics, those reports are not linked to you or your account, so we cannot find or delete them. Turn them off any time in Settings, Privacy and usage statistics. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).
