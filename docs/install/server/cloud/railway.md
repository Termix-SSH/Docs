## Installation

### Requirements:
- [Railway Account](https://railway.com/account)
- [Railway Plan](https://railway.com/pricing) (includes free 30 day free trial with $5 in credits)

After obtaining your railway account, visit the template link above to deploy now.

Once your project has been created, your Termix instance will be available at the URL provided by Railway. You can get that URL by clicking on the `lukegus/termix:latest` deployment and copying the URL given to you in the deployments tab.

Your Termix instance is now ready for setup.

### Setup:

Enter the URL from above into any browser, and create your account.

For a list of all Termix features, visit [docs](https://docs.termix.site/features)!

To change or add any [environment variables](https://docs.termix.site/environment-variables), click on the `lukegus/termix:latest` again, visit the `Variables` tab, and add variables as needed. 

## Support

If you need help or want to request a feature with Termix, visit the [Issues](https://github.com/Termix-SSH/Support/issues) page, log in, and press `New Issue`.
Please be as detailed as possible in your issue, preferably written in English. You can also join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/jVQGdvHDrf) server and visit the support
channel, however, response times may be longer.
